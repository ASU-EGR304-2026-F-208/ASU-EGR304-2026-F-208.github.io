## Project Objective

This project aims to address the instability experienced by workers who require a reliable workspace in changing environments. While self-leveling technology has been implemented in products such as commercial tables in restaurants, our product focuses specifically on mobile and all-terrain work environments where uneven surfaces can interfere with productivity and safety. The target users include workers who operate in environments such as construction sites, boats, and other areas where a stable workspace may not be readily available. By adapting existing self-leveling technologies into a portable and user-focused workstation, we aim to provide users with a dependable work surface without requiring them to modify or stabilize their surroundings. Our product is intended to improve the efficiency and convenience of working in unpredictable environments while maintaining ease of use and reliability across a variety of applications.

## Stakeholders

* **Target group** Upper-lower income mobile professionals of any age in unstable environments.
* **Target purchaser** Leads or advisors purchasing productivity-enhancing tools.
* **Customer service** Leans towards easy to repair, recyclable, and intuitively replaceable parts.
* **Marketing & Sales division** Looks for unique real world events to demonstrate active stabilization.
* **Retailers** E-commerce sites that can provide reliable shipping to independent customers.

## Use Cases

### User Story #1: Jenna

Sara has been working on a fishing boat as a technician for the last several months. As she has worked on the boat, she has had to do multiple projects in which she must work with small electronics. Now this is an Alaskan fishing boat, prone to many sea storms. A common occurrence that happens to Sara is the loss of small electronics due to the swaying of the ship. This leads to a non-negligible amount of product loss that puts her position on the ropes. Sara hears about our product and decides to give it a shot. 

While in the middle of a sea storm, our product keeps her workbench steady, causing her not to lose any of her small electronics. This works so well that her budget is evaluated because her loss of product has gone down so much. She now gets her second lunch break in her 12 hour shift.

### User Story #2

Jonathan is a gentleman of many hobbies. One of them is tornado chasing and photographing the tornadoes as he chases them. Now it is not uncommon that e has to switch out lenses, or deal with small attachments to his camera. Before pieces would fly everywhere. His driver would get pelted occasionally by a flash attachment. Jonathan then grabbed this nifty new self balancing workbench to keep in the back of the van while they chased tornadoes. His driver can now live with no stress with the knowledge that his attachments will not be hitting her in the face anymore, as this new workbench balances as they are driving to go after the tornado. 

Jonathan appreciates the table's ability to handle quick movements and adjust appropriately. This is a hard appreciation to get from Jonathan as he is a photographer and he is used to self stabilizing handles.

## Aspects

1. Hardware / Product Design (Outsourced to external designer)
   * 1.1 The product shall support static and dynamic payloads up to 10 lbs placed anywhere on the top platform surface without structural failure or frame deflection exceeding 2.0 mm. (P8)
   * 1.2 The outer casing shall enclose all mechanical drive mechanisms to maintain a modern, self-contained aesthetic suitable for vehicle and marine interiors. (P7)

2. Software / Functionality
   * 2.1 The system shall actively control platform orientation to maintain level within 1.0° of true horizontal during base motion. (P10)
   * 2.2 The control loop software shall process sensor inputs and actuate position correction with a maximum overall system latency of 25 ms. (P10)

3. Interactivity & User Experience
   * 3.1 The system interface shall feature a single primary control button to manage power states and initiate zero-point calibration. (P5)

4. Customization
   * 4.1 The system shall provide a tool-less quick-release mounting mechanism to transition between a cushioned lap base and a rigid standing mount. (P9)

5. Manufacturing
   * 5.1 The complete product assembly shall be designed for manufacturability to achieve a target Bill of Materials (BOM) cost of ~$200 per unit at moderate production volumes. (P7)

6. Safety
   * 6.1 The physical enclosure shall eliminate all mechanical pinch hazards during active motion. (P10)
   * 6.2 The system software and electrical controls shall automatically disconnect drive power upon detecting an over-tilt or over-load condition. (P10)



## Requirement Criteria Specifications

* 1.1.1 - The device shall collapse into a stowed position for easy storage and include a mechanical lock for transportation.
* 1.1.2 - Provide active stabilization for up to 3 hours with 5lbs of payload.
