## Team Introduction

> Team 208 consists of Joseph Nunez, Emma Cumiford, and Caden Laughlin. We are all
> engineering students working on the development of a self-stabilizing workbench
> using 3 PIC Micro-controllers, Sensors, and Actuators. The product will consist of
> an Inertial Measurement Unit and a Distance Sensor for Angle and Velocity Estimation
> and a Motor for Stabilizing Actuation. The workbench will sense changes in the surface's
> orientation and actively correcting itself in order to maintain a stable surface. This allows
> the practice of precise operations in unstable environments.
