## Team Charter

> "Team 208 is a collection of motivated undergraduate Engineering students aiming towards the development of a Self Stabilizing Work Bench. It will enhance the productivity professionals and consumers who find themselves working in unstable environments. "

Our interest towards a ***sensitive controls project*** brought us to the idea of ***stabilization***. After considering the possible scenarios, environments, and jobs that include factors of instability,
we recognized the need of a steady and reliable surface to conduct work on. This realization brought us to the concept of ***productivity*** optimization as a stable surface allows for more efficient
use of time in locations where a sturdy surface is not available.


## Product Mission Statement

 > "Team 208's mission is to aid the utility and efficiency of all professionals and consumers time spent working alike."

 Our mission reflects the goals of our group, as a whole. We wish to give those who find themselves in unstable environments, such as boats, all-terrain vehicles, or construction
 better ability to achieve tasks requiring a surface. Without the obstruction of an insecure workspace, one's time is better spent, ensuring the most possible value to a job, or passion,
 is deployed.
